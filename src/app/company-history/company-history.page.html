Our History

The journey of TripMate, from an idea to a nationwide platform.

TripMate is a proudly Filipino-built platform created by Group 10 at the Technological Institute of the Philippines (TIP) Quezon City. It was born out of a desire to make booking local stays — from hidden gems in Batanes to vibrant resorts in Siargao — easier, safer, and more connected to the communities they reside in.

Milestones

The Idea

Group 10 conceptualizes a booking app dedicated to the Philippines, prioritizing local payment methods and authentic experiences.

Prototype

Initial designs and the first functional prototype are built using Ionic and Angular, bringing the vision to life.

Launch

TripMate officially launches Version 1.0, connecting travelers with verified hosts across the archipelago.
